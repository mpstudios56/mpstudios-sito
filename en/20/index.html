MPStudios apparel

Series 20¹

Twenty MPStudios designs, printed on T-shirts and sweatshirts. Every piece carries its number, from 1/20¹ to 20/20¹.

Visit the Etsy shop

How the numbering works

  • A series is twenty designs, each with a fixed number: the wolf is 7/20¹, the lion 19/20¹.
  • The number is printed on the garment, next to the MPStudios signature.
  • When series 20¹ closes, series 20² comes out with twenty new designs. Closed series stay below, in the archive.
