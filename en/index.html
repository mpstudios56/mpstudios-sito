Android apps and designs

Android apps that don't ask you for an account

I'm Marcello Pandolfo. By day I work on telecom service governance for businesses; in the evening I build the apps I needed and couldn't find. Your data stays on your phone or in the cloud you choose. I never see it and never store it.

Apps

Three on Google Play, three on the way.

On Google Play

Cifra and Cifra+

Personal finance

Cifra is an Android personal finance app: accounts, transactions, budgets and reports stay on your phone. No account, no ads and no developer servers.

Cifra free; Cifra+ €5.99, one-time purchase
On Google Play

EMIA

Family health

EMIA is an Android app that keeps your whole family's lab reports, tests, treatments, vaccines, pregnancy, deadlines and appointments in order, pets included. Your data stays on your phone.

60-day free trial; from €14.99 a year or €29.99 lifetime
Coming to Google Play

Batonkey

Automatic notification forwarding

Batonkey is an Android app that reads the phone's notifications and forwards their content to the people you choose, following rules you set: by email automatically, even with the screen off, or by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram with one tap.

Host phone: free trial, then €59.99 a year; Guest phones: free
In development

Turbofuel

Motorbike and car maintenance

Turbofuel is an Android app for maintaining motorbikes, scooters, cars and vans: it shows what is fine, what is due soon and what is overdue, with service history, deadlines and vehicle documents.

Motorbikes and scooters free; cars and vans in the PRO version
In development

Benarri

Digital guest guide for holiday homes

Benarri is a digital guide for holiday home guests: Wi-Fi, keys, house rules, parking, contacts, buses and emergencies in five languages. It replaces the paper binder and long WhatsApp messages.

Benarri for guests: free; Benarri Host: paid

How the apps are built

Your data stays yours

On your phone, or in the Google Drive or cloud folder you choose. No developer servers.

No account

Install and use. PIN or fingerprint lock in the apps that handle personal data.

Five languages

Italian, English, French, Spanish and German.

Why each app exists

Cifra and Cifra+

I've kept track of household money for years. I wanted an app that logs payments by itself from bank notifications, without sending my transactions to a company.

EMIA

The whole family's lab reports, vaccines and deadlines were scattered across email, chats and drawers. When I needed a value, I couldn't find it.

Batonkey

At work some notifications land on a shared phone but are needed by a colleague. Copying them into a chat by hand wasted time, and the paid apps I tried stopped working after a few weeks.

Turbofuel

I look after my own vehicles and wanted to see at a glance what is due and what isn't, without a notebook in the glovebox.

Benarri

I run a B&B in Marina di Pisa. Every guest asked the same questions about Wi-Fi, keys and parking, and I answered each one on WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ ›

Who is behind MPStudios?

Marcello Pandolfo, an IT and telecom consultant. He designs, builds and publishes the apps on his own, under the MPStudios brand.

Do the apps ask me to create an account?

No. You install them from Google Play and use them straight away, without signing up.

Where does my data go?

On your phone and, if you turn it on, in the backup or cloud folder you choose (for example Google Drive or Dropbox). The developer never receives your data and has no servers that store it.

Do the apps work on iPhone?

Not at the moment: they are Android apps, distributed on Google Play.
