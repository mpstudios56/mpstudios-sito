Batonkey

Coming to Google Play

Batonkey is an Android app for businesses: a shared work phone receives login codes, portal alerts and alarms, and Batonkey forwards them at once to the right colleague, following rules you set.

Host phone

€59.99 a year, after the free trial

Guest phones

Free for colleagues

Screenshots

Forwarding rules

  • Create a rule from a notification you already received: the keyword fills in by itself.
  • One or more recipients, each on their own channel: email and SMS automatically, WhatsApp and Telegram with one tap.
  • If the notification holds a code, the code is sent; otherwise the notification text.
  • Rule priority, verified senders, recipient groups and active hours per weekday.

Host and Guest

  • Host: the phone that receives and forwards. Guest: the colleague's phone, always free.
  • Pairing with a personal, single-use QR code valid for 24 hours.
  • Booking: a colleague taps the provider and, for 5 adjustable minutes, that provider's next message goes only to them.
  • Per-colleague statistics: bookings, messages received, most used provider.

Control and privacy

  • Log of sent messages with time, sender, text and recipient.
  • Alert if notification access stops.
  • PIN or fingerprint lock. No account, no developer servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is Batonkey for?

It gets the login codes and alerts that reach a shared work phone to the person who needs them, without anyone picking up that phone.

How much does it cost?

The Host phone has a free trial, then costs €59.99 a year. Colleagues' Guest phones are always free.

Which channels does it use?

Email and SMS automatically; WhatsApp and Telegram with one tap.

How do I stop a code reaching the wrong person?

With booking: a colleague taps the provider and for 5 adjustable minutes that provider's next message goes only to them.

Do messages go through a server?

No. Batonkey has no accounts and uses no developer servers.
