Batonkey

Coming to Google Play

Batonkey is an Android app that reads the phone's notifications and forwards their content to the people you choose, following rules you set: by email automatically, even with the screen off, or by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram with one tap.

Host phone

€59.99 a year, after the free trial

Guest phones

Free for colleagues

Screenshots

Forwarding rules

  • Create a rule from a notification you already received: the app fills in almost everything.
  • One or more recipients, from your contacts too, each on their own channel.
  • Add the sender's name or your own text.
  • A weekly schedule to forward only during working hours.

Host and Guest

  • Host: the phone that receives the notifications and forwards them. Guest: the colleague's phone, always free.
  • Pairing with a personal, single-use QR code valid for 24 hours.
  • Booking: a colleague taps the sender and, for 5 adjustable minutes, that sender's next notification goes only to them.

Control and privacy

  • Dashboard with forwarding statistics by recipient, sender and channel.
  • Alert if notification access stops.
  • PIN or fingerprint lock and backup of your rules.
  • No account and no developer servers: your email password and PIN stay on the phone.

Frequently asked questions

What is Batonkey for?

It forwards the content of a notification to the person who needs it, automatically, when the notification lands on a phone used by several people: an office, a shop, a family.

How much does it cost?

The Host phone has a free trial, then costs €59.99 a year. Colleagues' Guest phones are always free.

Which channels does it use?

Email automatically, even with the screen off; SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram with one tap.

Can I limit forwarding to working hours?

Yes, with the rules' weekly schedule.

Do notifications go through a server?

No. Batonkey reads notifications only on the phone and has no accounts or developer servers.

Version history

No public release yet: the app is in development.
