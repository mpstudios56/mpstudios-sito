Cifra and Cifra+

On Google Play

Cifra is an Android personal finance app: accounts, transactions, budgets and reports stay on your phone. No account, no ads and no developer servers.

Screenshots

Accounts and transactions

  • Current accounts, cards, cash, deposits and securities, credits and debts, each with its bank logo and colour.
  • Multiple currencies, with exchange rates on transfers.
  • Categories and subcategories, projects, places and payees.
  • Split transactions, recurring entries, templates, drafts, receipt photos and search on every field.

Overview, budgets and reports

  • Net worth, income, expenses and savings for the period; spending by category in a ring chart.
  • Budgets by category, project or account, with a green, amber or red bar.
  • Twelve reports for totals and trends, with bar, pie and line charts.

Backup and data

  • Full backup of data, photos and settings to the phone, Google Drive or Dropbox, manual or daily.
  • Import from other apps and export in CSV and QIF.
  • A Cifra+ backup can be restored on free Cifra too.

Only in Cifra+

  • Reads notifications from your bank and payment apps: the transaction is recorded on the right account automatically.
  • Rules that assign category, payee and project to transactions read from notifications.
  • Receipt scanning with the camera, with text recognition on the phone.
  • Instalment plans with «Move instalment».
  • Accounts shared between two people through a cloud folder you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cifra free?

Yes. Cifra is free and has no ads. Cifra+ costs €5.99 as a one-time purchase on Google Play, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. You install Cifra and use it without signing up.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Backups go where you choose: a file on the phone, Google Drive or Dropbox. The developer never receives your data.

What does Cifra+ add?

Automatic transactions from bank notifications, receipt scanning, instalment plans and accounts shared between two people.

Can I import transactions from another app?

Yes, from CSV and QIF files. Cifra exports in the same formats.
