Apps › Digital guest guide for holiday homes

Benarri

In development

Benarri is a digital guide for holiday home guests: Wi-Fi, keys, house rules, parking, contacts, buses and emergencies in five languages. It replaces the paper binder and long WhatsApp messages.

Benarri

Free for guests

Benarri Host

Paid for hosts

Screenshots

Benarri Host, for hosts

  • One guide per apartment, with its own logo and colours.
  • One-tap translation into English, German, French and Spanish, done on the phone.
  • Publish with a link or a QR code; stays with dates taken from the Airbnb or Booking calendar.
  • Messages to guests with read receipts and house-rule confirmation.

Benarri, for guests

  • The guide in the guest's language, readable offline.
  • Wi-Fi with QR code, contacts with call, WhatsApp and map, bus times, emergency numbers.
  • Guests who do not want to install the app open the same guide in the browser.

Where the data lives

  • In the host's Google Drive, in a dedicated folder. No developer servers.
  • Guests do not create accounts or sign up.

Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The guide also opens in the browser from the shared link; the Benarri app also works offline.

Which languages?

Italian, English, German, French and Spanish. Translation runs on the host's phone and is free.

Where is the data stored?

In the host's Google Drive, in a Benarri folder. There are no developer servers.

Does it work with Airbnb and Booking?

Benarri Host reads the booking calendar (iCal) to create stays with the right dates.

Version history

No public release yet: the app is in development.
