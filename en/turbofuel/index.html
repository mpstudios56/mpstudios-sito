Turbofuel

In development

Turbofuel is an Android app for maintaining motorbikes, scooters, cars and vans: it shows what is fine, what is due soon and what is overdue, with service history, deadlines and vehicle documents.

Motorbikes and scooters

Free

Cars and vans

PRO version

Screenshots

Dashboard and vehicles

  • Odometer, next service and the state of every part: oil, chain, tyres, brakes, filters.
  • Several garages, a full vehicle record and registration document read from a photo.
  • Mileage read with the camera, offline.

Service and deadlines

  • Services with parts, mileage, parts and labour costs, workshop and notes.
  • Road tax, insurance and inspection in the phone calendar, with alerts 30, 10, 3 and 1 day before.
  • Costs and consumption: cost per km, spending by category, full-to-full fuel consumption.

Tools

  • NFC tags on the vehicle to open the app or log mileage and services by tapping the phone.
  • Pre-ride checklist, track diary, workshop directory.
  • PDF service book for whoever buys the vehicle; CSV export.

Frequently asked questions

Which vehicles does Turbofuel support?

Motorbikes, scooters, cars and vans. Motorbikes and scooters are free; cars and vans are in the PRO version.

How does it log mileage?

With the camera: frame the odometer and confirm the number. It works offline.

Does it remind me about road tax, insurance and inspection?

Yes. Deadlines go into the phone calendar with alerts 30, 10, 3 and 1 day before.

Where is the data stored?

On the phone, with backups on the phone and on the user's Google Drive.

Version history

No public release yet: the app is in development.
