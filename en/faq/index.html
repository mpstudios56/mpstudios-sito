Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about MPStudios apps: accounts, data, languages, changing phone, prices and questions about each app.

General questions

Who is behind MPStudios?

Marcello Pandolfo, an IT and telecom consultant. He designs, builds and publishes the apps on his own, under the MPStudios brand.

Do the apps ask me to create an account?

No. You install them from Google Play and use them straight away, without signing up.

Where does my data go?

On your phone and, if you turn it on, in the backup or cloud folder you choose (for example Google Drive or Dropbox). The developer never receives your data and has no servers that store it.

Do the apps work on iPhone?

Not at the moment: they are Android apps, distributed on Google Play.

Which languages are available?

Italian, English, Spanish, French and German.

What happens if I change phone?

Make a backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one. Purchases made on Google Play stay linked to your Google account: reinstall the app with the same account and they are there.

How do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Write to the email address on the About page. I answer myself, and reports end up in the updates.

Why are some apps paid?

I don't sell data and don't use servers: the price pays for development and updates. Cifra has a free version; EMIA and Batonkey have a free trial.

When will Batonkey, Turbofuel and Benarri be out?

They are in development. When they reach Google Play, their pages on this site will carry the link and the version history.

Cifra and Cifra+ · Personal finance

Is Cifra free?

Yes. Cifra is free and has no ads. Cifra+ costs €5.99 as a one-time purchase on Google Play, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. You install Cifra and use it without signing up.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Backups go where you choose: a file on the phone, Google Drive or Dropbox. The developer never receives your data.

What does Cifra+ add?

Automatic transactions from bank notifications, receipt scanning, instalment plans and accounts shared between two people.

Can I import transactions from another app?

Yes, from CSV and QIF files. Cifra exports in the same formats.

EMIA · Family health

How much does EMIA cost?

The first 60 days are free with all features. Then EMIA costs €14.99 a year or €29.99 lifetime for one person; EMIA Family €29.99 a year or €59.99 lifetime; EMIA Family Plus €39.99 a year or €79.99 lifetime.

Does EMIA diagnose?

No. EMIA organises data and flags values outside reference ranges. Interpreting results is up to your doctor.

Where does my health data go?

It stays on your phone, in the backup you choose and, if you turn it on, in the family's shared folder. EMIA has no accounts and no servers.

Can I manage the whole family and pets?

Yes. EMIA Family covers the whole family; EMIA Family Plus adds pets and the family on several phones.

Where do the reference ranges come from?

From official bodies such as WHO, the Italian National Institute of Health, the Italian Ministry of Health, AIFA and EMA, cited in the app.

Batonkey · Automatic notification forwarding

What is Batonkey for?

It forwards the content of a notification to the person who needs it, automatically, when the notification lands on a phone used by several people: an office, a shop, a family.

How much does it cost?

The Host phone has a free trial, then costs €59.99 a year. Colleagues' Guest phones are always free.

Which channels does it use?

Email automatically, even with the screen off; SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram with one tap.

Can I limit forwarding to working hours?

Yes, with the rules' weekly schedule.

Do notifications go through a server?

No. Batonkey reads notifications only on the phone and has no accounts or developer servers.

Turbofuel · Motorbike and car maintenance

Which vehicles does Turbofuel support?

Motorbikes, scooters, cars and vans. Motorbikes and scooters are free; cars and vans are in the PRO version.

How does it log mileage?

With the camera: frame the odometer and confirm the number. It works offline.

Does it remind me about road tax, insurance and inspection?

Yes. Deadlines go into the phone calendar with alerts 30, 10, 3 and 1 day before.

Where is the data stored?

On the phone, with backups on the phone and on the user's Google Drive.

Benarri · Digital guest guide for holiday homes

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The guide also opens in the browser from the shared link; the Benarri app also works offline.

Which languages?

Italian, English, German, French and Spanish. Translation runs on the host's phone and is free.

Where is the data stored?

In the host's Google Drive, in a Benarri folder. There are no developer servers.

Does it work with Airbnb and Booking?

Benarri Host reads the booking calendar (iCal) to create stays with the right dates.
