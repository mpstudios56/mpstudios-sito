EMIA

On Google Play

EMIA is an Android app that keeps your whole family's lab reports, tests, treatments, vaccines, pregnancy, deadlines and appointments in order, pets included. Your data stays on your phone.

Try it free for 60 daysAll features included in the trial

Screenshots

Lab reports and tests

  • Add reports from PDF, image, camera or by hand, with values read on the phone.
  • For every value: chart, reference range, history and comparison between two reports.
  • Reference ranges from official sources when the report does not include them.

Measurements, treatments and medicines

  • Weight, blood pressure, oxygen, glucose, temperature and heart rate; WHO growth curves for children.
  • Treatments with reminders at the exact time, doses to confirm and history.
  • Medicine recognition from the package and lookup in the Italian AIFA drug database.

Family, vaccines and deadlines

  • Vaccination record with the national and regional schedule.
  • Pregnancy, cycle, blood donations, teeth, eyesight, exemptions and paperwork.
  • Every deadline becomes an appointment in the Agenda and in the phone calendar, with alerts 30, 10 and 3 days before and on the day.
  • Family on several phones through a shared folder you choose.

Emergency

  • Emergency card for every person and pet, readable offline and from the lock screen.
  • Blood type, allergies, conditions, treatments, contacts and a button to call the country's emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

How much does EMIA cost?

The first 60 days are free with all features. Then EMIA costs €14.99 a year or €29.99 lifetime for one person; EMIA Family €29.99 a year or €59.99 lifetime; EMIA Family Plus €39.99 a year or €79.99 lifetime.

Does EMIA diagnose?

No. EMIA organises data and flags values outside reference ranges. Interpreting results is up to your doctor.

Where does my health data go?

It stays on your phone, in the backup you choose and, if you turn it on, in the family's shared folder. EMIA has no accounts and no servers.

Can I manage the whole family and pets?

Yes. EMIA Family covers the whole family; EMIA Family Plus adds pets and the family on several phones.

Where do the reference ranges come from?

From official bodies such as WHO, the Italian National Institute of Health, the Italian Ministry of Health, AIFA and EMA, cited in the app.

Version history

Releases published to production on Google Play.

1.4.0 · 20 November 2026
  • Every deadline you enter (exemptions, disability paperwork, contact lenses, treatment medicines) becomes an appointment in the Agenda and in the phone calendar, with alerts 30, 10 and 3 days before and on the day.

  • Lifestyle and cancer screening by age, sex and country of residence.
