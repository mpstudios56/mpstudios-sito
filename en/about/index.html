About

Marcello Pandolfo

IT and telecom consultant with a degree in economics. I lead a governance team serving telecom and IT companies.

MPStudios is the brand under which I publish the apps and designs I build outside my day job. Each app started from a need I had myself: tracking household money, organising the family's medical reports, passing a shared phone's notifications to a colleague, keeping up with vehicle maintenance, giving holiday-home guests a clear guide.

The same rules apply to all of them: no account, no developer servers, data on the phone or in the cloud the user chooses.

I have a degree in economics. I work in business telecoms consulting, leading the team that checks mobile and fixed-line invoices, contracts and costs on behalf of clients.

With my wife I run a B&B in Marina di Pisa, and Benarri is the guide I wanted to give our guests. In my spare time I paint acrylic miniatures and post them on the MASart YouTube channel.

I write the apps on my own, publish them on Google Play as MPStudios and update them based on what users report. If you have a question or a problem with an app, write to me: I answer myself.

The apps

  • Cifra and Cifra+: personal finance
  • EMIA: family health
  • Batonkey: automatic notification forwarding
  • Turbofuel: motorbike and car maintenance
  • Benarri: digital guest guide for holiday homes
