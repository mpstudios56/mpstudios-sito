About

Marcello Pandolfo

IT and telecom consultant with a degree in economics. I lead a governance team serving telecom and IT companies.

MPStudios is the brand under which I publish the apps and designs I build outside my day job. Each app started from a need I had myself: tracking household money, organising the family's medical reports, routing login codes from a shared work phone, keeping up with vehicle maintenance, giving holiday-home guests a clear guide.

The same rules apply to all of them: no account, no developer servers, data on the phone or in the cloud the user chooses.

The apps

  • Cifra and Cifra+: personal finance
  • EMIA: family health
  • Batonkey: notification forwarding for teams
  • Turbofuel: motorbike and car maintenance
  • Benarri: digital guest guide for holiday homes
