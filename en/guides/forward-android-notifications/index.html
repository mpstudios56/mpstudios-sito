How to forward an Android phone's notifications to another person automatically

by Marcello Pandolfo, updated 21 November 2026

To send the content of an Android notification to another person automatically, you need an app with notification access that forwards it by email or message, following rules per sender. Then nobody has to pick up the shared phone.

When you need it

  • In an office or shop, one phone receives alerts meant for a colleague.
  • In a family, one phone receives confirmations and alerts the other parent also needs.
  • The person concerned does not have the phone at hand.

Manual workarounds and their limits

  • Reading the message and retyping it in a chat only works if someone is there.
  • A screenshot stays in the gallery and in the chat history.
  • Leaving the phone in a shared spot means nobody knows who read what.

What automatic forwarding must do

  • Read the notification on the phone, with the notification access you grant.
  • Apply a rule per sender: which notifications, to whom, on which channel.
  • Forward only during the hours you decide.
  • Keep a log of what was sent, without external servers.

How Batonkey does it

  • Rules are created from a notification you already received.
  • Email automatically, even with the screen off; SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram with one tap.
  • Weekly schedule, forwarding statistics, PIN or fingerprint lock.
  • No account and no developer servers.
