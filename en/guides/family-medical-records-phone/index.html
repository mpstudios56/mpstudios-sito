Your family's lab reports, treatments and health deadlines: keeping them in order on your phone

by Marcello Pandolfo · Updated 21 November 2026

Keeping the whole family's lab reports, treatments and deadlines in order on a phone takes one record per person, test values you can read over time, and every deadline turned into a calendar reminder.

One place per person

  • PDF reports, photos of prescriptions, vaccines and treatments often end up in different chats, emails and drawers.
  • One record per person, pets included, lets you find everything in seconds, even at the doctor's.

Comparing values across reports

  • A single out-of-range value says little; the trend across several reports says more.
  • You need a chart for each value with the lab's reference range or, when missing, one from an official source.

Every deadline becomes a reminder

  • Vaccine boosters, exemption renewals, dental check-ups, contact lens boxes: every date goes into the phone calendar.
  • Alerts a few weeks and a few days ahead stop you noticing the day after.

Privacy first

  • Health data is the most sensitive kind: better an app that keeps it on the phone and asks for no account.
  • Backups should go where you choose.

How EMIA does it

  • A record for every person and pet, with reports read from PDF or camera.
  • Chart, reference range and comparison between two reports for every value.
  • Every deadline goes into the Agenda and the phone calendar with alerts 30, 10 and 3 days ahead and on the day.
  • No account and no server; EMIA does not diagnose, interpreting results is up to your doctor.
