A guest guide for your holiday home: what to write and how to hand it over

by Marcello Pandolfo · Updated 21 November 2026

A good guest guide answers questions before they arrive: Wi-Fi, keys, house rules, parking, waste, transport and emergencies. Send it before arrival, in the guest's language, and make sure it works offline.

What every guest asks

  • The Wi-Fi name and password.
  • How to get in: keys, key box, door code.
  • Where to park and how much it costs.
  • How to sort the rubbish.
  • What time to check out.

What order to put it in

  • Arrival first: address, access, parking.
  • Then the home: Wi-Fi, appliances, rules.
  • Then the area: transport, shops, beach or town centre.
  • Emergencies last: useful numbers and your contact.

Paper or digital

  • A paper binder stays in the home and guests only see it after they arrive.
  • A digital guide is sent as a link before the trip and updated in a minute.
  • When the guide is in the guest's language, questions drop.

How Benarri does it

  • One guide per apartment, with its own logo and colours.
  • Translation into English, German, French and Spanish done on the host's phone.
  • Guests open it from a link or QR code, in the browser too, and offline.
  • The data stays in the host's Google Drive.
