Expense tracking apps with no account and no server: what to check before you pick one

by Marcello Pandolfo, updated 21 November 2026

An expense app that asks for no account and uses no server keeps your transactions only on your phone. Before choosing one, check where backups go, whether it imports and exports open formats, and how it records transactions without typing each one.

1. Where does the data go?

  • If the app asks you to create an account, your transactions are also stored on the developer's servers.
  • If it doesn't, the data stays on the phone and backups are your responsibility.

2. How do backups work?

  • Check that a backup includes everything: transactions, receipt photos and settings.
  • Better if you can choose the destination (phone file, Google Drive, Dropbox) and run it daily.

3. Can you import and export?

  • CSV and QIF are read by almost every personal finance app and by spreadsheets.
  • An app that only exports to its own format makes it hard to switch later.

4. Do I have to type every transaction?

  • On Android an app can read bank notifications, with the notification access you grant, and log the transaction by itself.
  • Make sure the rules can be edited, so category, payee and account are right from the start.

5. What does it really cost?

  • Free with ads, monthly subscription or one-time purchase are three different models.
  • With a one-time purchase you pay once and the feature stays yours.
