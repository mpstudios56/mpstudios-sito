Road tax, insurance, inspection and servicing: never miss a car or motorbike deadline again

by Marcello Pandolfo, updated 21 November 2026

Every car or motorbike has four deadlines that come back at fixed intervals: road tax, insurance, inspection and servicing. The simplest way not to miss one is to note the next date as soon as you pay or have the work done, with a reminder a few weeks ahead.

The four deadlines

  • Road tax: paid every year, in a month that depends on registration or the last payment.
  • Insurance: usually lasts a year; the date is on the policy.
  • Inspection: in Italy the first comes four years after registration, then every two years.
  • Servicing: every so many kilometres or months, according to the manufacturer's handbook.

Why they get forgotten

  • They fall in different months with different rules.
  • Servicing depends on mileage, which nobody checks until a warning light comes on.
  • With several vehicles in the family, the dates multiply.

A method that works

  • As soon as you pay or have the work done, note the next deadline.
  • Set one alert a month ahead and one a few days ahead.
  • Log mileage at every fill-up or service, so you know how far the next one is.
  • Keep photos of the registration document, policy and receipts in one place.
